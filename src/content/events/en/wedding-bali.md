The ceremony was set on a cliffside overlooking the water, chairs arranged in loose rows beneath a canopy of white flowers. As guests arrived in the late afternoon heat, the light was already turning soft and low — the kind that asks very little of a photographer and gives everything back.

What stayed with me most was the pace of the day: unhurried, warm, carried along by the sound of the tide below. Between the formal moments there were long stretches of quiet — hands held under a table, a shared look across the room — and those are the frames I kept returning to.

By the time the first dance began, the sky had gone the color of dusk and the sea had disappeared into it. A celebration built for two people, photographed to feel like exactly that.
